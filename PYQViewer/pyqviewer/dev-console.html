What this console can and can't do

This console has full control over everything inside the app: every teacher account (approve, reject, promote, force-logout, delete), every subject and worksheet, and the site's public on/off state. What it deliberately does not do is reach outside the app — it can't redeploy or modify the Vercel project, edit environment variables/secrets, or touch the GitHub repo. Wiring that in would mean a public login form having write-access to your entire hosting account, which is a much bigger risk than anything this app needs to solve. If you ever do want a "restart the deployment" button, that's a separate, deliberate step (a scoped Vercel API token) — say the word and it can be added on top of this.
